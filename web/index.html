Free and open source · Windows 11

Your XREAL glasses, as a multi-monitor desk

XrealScreen turns XREAL 1S glasses into extra Windows monitors. Drag windows onto them as usual. Keep them still in space or let them follow your head.

Download View on GitHub

MIT license · No account · No telemetry

How it works

Plug in, start, work

No extra hardware. The glasses connect with one USB-C cable, and XrealScreen does the rest.

Connect the glasses

Video arrives as a normal Windows monitor. Head tracking arrives over the glasses' USB network link.

Windows gets real monitors

XrealScreen adds virtual monitors with an open-source display driver. Windows treats them like any other screen.

See them in the glasses

The screens are drawn on the glasses with Direct3D 11: split pixel-exact, or placed in 3D around you.

Two workspace types

Rock-steady or all around you

Pick the type that suits your work. Switch any time on the Screens page.

Most stable

Glasses anchor

The glasses hold their own UltraWide image still in space. XrealScreen splits it into 1–3 pixel-exact screens.

  • UltraWide 32:9, 21:9 or 16:18
  • Adjustable gap and screen shape
  • Restarts by itself when you change UltraWide
Up to 6 screens

App head tracking

Up to six curved screens in an arc or a grid, tracked by the app from the glasses' motion sensors.

  • Stabilizer presets and tracking axes
  • Neck model and auto center with a dead zone
  • Adjustable distance (Ctrl+Alt++/−)
Features

Built for real work

Real virtual monitors

Windows sees extra monitors, so every app works. Your layout and refresh rates come back when you stop, even after a crash.

Saved workspaces

Save, load, import and export your setups. All settings save automatically.

Automatic start

Start a chosen workspace with XrealScreen or as soon as you plug in the glasses.

Glasses screenshots

Save exactly what the glasses show with Ctrl+Alt+P.

Head look for games

Steer games with your head: head movement becomes mouse movement, with no driver. Tested in Skyrim.

For developers

A .NET SDK for head tracking, and a local API that lets any language control the running app.

Primary monitor

Make any virtual screen the Windows primary monitor while the workspace runs.

Tray and hotkeys

Runs quietly in the tray. Ctrl+Alt+W starts and stops the workspace.

Four looks

Stage, Lens, Instrument or Classic. Switch any time under Settings → Look.

Four looks

Make it yours

Each look has its own Home page. Pick one under Settings → Look.

Stage (default)

Your screens in a dark room at their real angles, with every action in one dock.

Lens

A round Start button with live head readouts.

Instrument

A light, precise look with a field map on a degree scale.

Classic

The standard Windows look: Fluent design, Mica, light or dark.
